About the dictionary: how to use it, history, sources and licences.

What it is and how to use it

atbulinis.lt is a free online reverse dictionary of Lithuanian. It contains 202,780 words – common words and proper names (first names, surnames, place names) – sorted from the end of the word in Lithuanian alphabetical order.

  • Ends with / Starts with / Contains – words with the given letters at the end, start or anywhere (e.g. -tuvas, -uva).
  • Pattern – for crosswords: ? is any letter, * any number of letters.
  • Anagrams – words that can be made from the given letters.
  • Rhymes – rhymes using all inflected forms (about 4.4 million), grouped by syllables, plus near rhymes.
  • Filters – part of speech, frequency, length, common words or specific kinds of proper names (first names, surnames, place names, hydronyms).
  • Word card – click a word to see its entry from open dictionaries, its morphemes, its kind and links to other dictionaries.

The whole dictionary can be downloaded: PDF, TXT (aligned), TXT (list).

History

It began with curiosity about the origin of the word Lietuva (Lithuania): I wanted to find as many place names (and other words) ending in -uva as possible. I found no help online and did not feel like going to a library to leaf through a printed book – so the idea of an online reverse dictionary was born.

Earlier Lithuanian reverse dictionaries were published in print: David F. Robinson’s Lithuanian Reverse Dictionary (1976), Vida Žilinskienė’s Atgalinis dabartinės lietuvių kalbos žodynas (1995; 74,081 words), the Reverse Dictionary of Lithuanian Morphemics by E. Rimkutė, A. Kazlauskienė and G. Raškinis (VDU, 2011) and Alvydas Butkus’s reverse dictionary of Lithuanian nicknames (2009).
